What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember information about your visit — such as your login session or display preferences. Some cookies are strictly necessary for the site to function; others are optional and used to improve your experience or help us understand how the platform is used.

Manage Your Preferences

Toggle each category below. Changes are saved to your browser and take effect immediately. You can update your preferences at any time by returning to this page.

Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary for the platform to function and cannot be disabled. They enable core features such as user authentication, session management, and security protections. No personal data collected by these cookies is used for tracking or advertising.


Examples: session ID, CSRF token, login state

Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your display and personalisation choices so you don't have to reset them each visit — for example, your dark or light mode preference.


Examples: theme setting (dark/light mode), language preference

Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the platform so we can improve it. All data collected is anonymised and aggregated — we cannot identify you individually from analytics data.


Examples: pages visited, features used, session duration

Marketing & Advertising Cookies

South African Teachers Hub does not use any marketing, retargeting, or advertising cookies. We do not track you across other websites and we do not share your data with advertising networks.

More Information

  4. For more information on how we use your personal information, see our Privacy Policy and POPIA Compliance pages.
