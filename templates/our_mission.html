Our Mission

Built by a teacher.
For every teacher who deserves their evenings back.

South African Teachers Hub exists for one reason: so South African educators can stop drowning in admin and get back to the work that drew them into the classroom in the first place.

A note from the founder

I spent 17 years as a teacher and facilitator.

I know what it feels like to lose a Sunday to a question paper that should have taken an hour. To rewrite the same instructions you wrote last term, because last term's file is on a laptop you don't use anymore. To open a memo at 11pm and realise it's the wrong version. To send a paper to your HOD and spend three days bouncing tracked-changes back and forth before it's finally approved.

And it's never as simple as just reusing last year's paper. Every year is different. Some terms you don't get through every topic. Some terms you cover more than the year before. So you sit down to recycle the old paper and realise half of it tests work your class never reached, and a chunk of what they did learn isn't on it at all. Which means editing question by question, rebuilding the memo to match, re-checking the mark allocation, and often just starting from a blank page anyway — on a Sunday night, on a laptop, on your own time.

I know what that quietly takes from you. By Monday, the lesson you'd hoped to plan a little better gets done from memory. The learner you meant to check in with waits another week. The marking you wanted to give real feedback on gets a tick and a number. You still show up — teachers always do — but a little of the joy is left behind in the admin.

South African Teachers Hub is the tool I wish I'd had. A quiet workspace where every paper, memo, rubric and assessment lives in one place — CAPS-aligned, easy to edit when the year takes a different shape, and ready to hand to the next teacher who walks into your classroom. So that the part of teaching you actually love is the part you get to spend your time on.

— The South African Teachers Hub team

Data integrity, safety & privacy

Your assessments stay yours. Always.

The papers you build are intellectual property — yours and your school's. South African Teachers Hub treats them that way. Encrypted at rest, locked behind your account, never used to train anything, never sold, never shared.

  • Encrypted & access-controlled

    Every file encrypted at rest. Role-based access so only the right people in your school can see, edit or moderate.

  • POPIA-compliant by design

    Hosted in line with South African data-protection law. Learner data minimised. Audit trails for every change.

  • Versioned & backed up

    Daily backups, point-in-time restore. Nothing lost to a hard drive failure or an accidentally-deleted folder.

  • Never used to train AI. Ever.

    Your papers and memos are not training data. Not for us, not for anyone. They exist for your school and only your school.

  • Yours to take with you

    Export every paper, memo and rubric at any time, in standard formats. No lock-in — if you leave, your work leaves with you.
