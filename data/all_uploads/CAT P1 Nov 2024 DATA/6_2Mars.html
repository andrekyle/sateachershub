Living on Mars

Could we really live on Mars?

The possibility of life on Mars is a subject of interest in astrobiology due to the planet's proximity and similarities to Earth.

WHY MARS?

Mars is one of Earth's closest habitable neighbours. Mars is about half as far from the Sun as Earth is, so it still has decent sunlight.

Diameter - 6 791 km

Gravity - 38% of Earth

Distance - 225 M km
